Prequalification snapshot.

A quick view of our eligibility for solar EPC, government and institutional tenders. Detailed documents and certificates are shared on request after initial qualification.

Types of tenders we qualify for
Solar EPC — ground-mount, rooftop, utility-scale & C&I. Empanelled / eligible under MSME, RRECL, PM-KUSUM, PM Surya Ghar, A-Grade Electrical, PWD (Electrical) and Urban Development works.
Minimum project-size capability
From 3 KW (residential) to 1 MW+ (PM-KUSUM) and utility-scale EPC — 300 MW delivered.
Turnover eligibility
₹12 Crore annual turnover.
Similar-work experience
300 MW EPC executed; projects at Napasar, Nawalgarh and other sites across Rajasthan; 200+ projects delivered since 2018.
Available technical staff
In-house engineering team — B.Tech, M.Tech & Diploma engineers (Electrical & Renewable Energy), backed by an 85+ execution workforce.
Required licences
A-Grade Electrical Contractor · PWD (Electrical) · MSME / Udyam · RRECL · GST (08AFHFS4941J1ZK).
EMD / security-deposit capacity
EMD and bank-guarantee capacity available for eligible tenders.
