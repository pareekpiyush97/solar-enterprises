What's included

Where every rupee actually goes.

A rooftop system is six things, not one. Knowing the split is the fastest way to judge whether a cheap quote is a bargain or a corner being cut.

Solar panels · ~50%

Mono PERC or TOPCon modules with a 25-year performance warranty. The single largest line item.

Inverter · ~15%

On-grid inverter with 5–10 year warranty. Where cheap quotes cut first, and where failures show up in year four.

Mounting structure · ~12%

Hot-dip galvanised structure rated for Rajasthan wind loads. Under-specify this and the array is at risk in a storm.

Cabling & protection · ~10%

DC/AC cable, ACDB/DCDB, earthing and lightning arrestor. Invisible from the ground, critical for safety.

Installation · ~8%

Our own certified crew. We do not sub-contract the install to whoever is free that week.

Paperwork · ~5%

DISCOM application, net metering and the full PM Surya Ghar subsidy filing — handled end to end.

Comparing quotes

Why one 5 KW quote is ₹2.85L and another is ₹2.10L.

If you are holding three quotes with three different numbers, these are the five questions that explain the gap. Ask every company the same five — including us.

  1. 01

    Which panel, exactly?

    Brand, wattage, technology and whether it's DCR — required for subsidy eligibility.

  2. 02

    Which inverter, and what warranty?

    A 5-year inverter in a 25-year system means a replacement you haven't budgeted for.

  3. 03

    What gauge is the structure?

    Ask for the specification in writing. This is the most common silent downgrade.

  4. 04

    Is net metering included?

    If DISCOM liaison is billed separately later, the quote you're comparing isn't complete.

  5. 05

    Who comes back in year three?

    The cheapest installer is often the one least likely to still be trading. Ask how long they've been in business.

How much does solar panel installation cost in 2026?

For an on-grid rooftop system, expect roughly ₹55,000 to ₹65,000 per KW installed. A 3 KW home system is around ₹1.80 Lakh before subsidy and about ₹1.02 Lakh after the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy. A 5 KW system is around ₹2.85 Lakh before subsidy and about ₹2.07 Lakh after. The price per KW falls as the system gets bigger.

What is the price of a 3 KW solar system?

Around ₹1.80 Lakh installed. A 3 KW system qualifies for the maximum residential subsidy of ₹78,000, bringing the net cost to roughly ₹1.02 Lakh. In Rajasthan it generates about 360 units a month — enough to cover a household bill of around ₹2,900.

What is the price of a 5 KW solar system?

Around ₹2.85 Lakh installed, or about ₹2.07 Lakh after the ₹78,000 subsidy. It generates roughly 600 units a month in Rajasthan, covering a bill of about ₹4,800 a month.

Is the subsidy deducted from the price I pay?

No. You pay the full system price, and the government credits the subsidy directly into your bank account after installation and DISCOM inspection. If a company quotes you a price with the subsidy already deducted, that is not the amount you will actually need on day one — ask them to restate it.

How long does a solar system take to pay for itself?

In Rajasthan, a subsidised residential system typically pays back in about 3 to 4 years. A commercial system without subsidy typically pays back in 3.5 to 5 years, and faster once accelerated depreciation is applied. Panels carry a 25-year performance warranty, so the two decades after payback are effectively free electricity.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Residential rooftop systems are eligible for low-interest, collateral-free bank loans under the PM Surya Ghar scheme. For most households the monthly EMI works out lower than the electricity bill the system replaces. Ask us and we'll help you arrange it alongside the subsidy paperwork.

Do prices differ in Bikaner compared to the rest of India?

Hardware costs are broadly national. What differs is generation — Bikaner and western Rajasthan get among the strongest sunlight in the country, so the same system produces more units here than it would in most other states. Identical price, shorter payback.
