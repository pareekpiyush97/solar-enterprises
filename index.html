Questions, answered

Solar in Bikaner — your questions.

Still unsure? Call our team on +91 98296 41110 for a free, no-pressure consultation.

How much does a solar system cost in Bikaner?

Residential rooftop systems (3–20 KW) start from ₹1.80 Lakh, and with the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy your effective cost is even lower. Commercial and industrial systems (5–200 KW) start from ₹3.50 Lakh. Exact pricing depends on your roof, load and system size — we share a clear, itemised quote after a free site survey.

What government subsidy can I get on rooftop solar?

Homeowners can claim up to ₹78,000 under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana for residential rooftop solar. As a registered vendor, we handle the entire subsidy and DISCOM application for you, end to end.

How much can solar reduce my electricity bill?

Most of our customers cut their electricity bills by up to 90%. With net metering, the surplus power your system generates is exported to the grid and adjusted against what you consume.

How long does installation take?

A typical residential system is installed in 2–5 days once materials and approvals are ready. Larger commercial projects vary by size. The full journey — survey, design, subsidy paperwork, installation and net metering — usually completes within a few weeks.

What warranty and lifespan do the panels have?

Solar panels are built to perform for 25 years, with manufacturer warranties on the panels and inverter. We also provide 6 months of on-site support after installation and remain available for service well beyond that.

Do you handle net metering and government paperwork?

Yes. From consultation and system design to installation, we manage net metering, DISCOM approvals and the complete subsidy paperwork — so you don't have to.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Bikaner and serve homes, businesses and institutions across Bikaner and the wider Rajasthan region, including nearby Nagaur, Churu, Sri Ganganagar and Jodhpur.
