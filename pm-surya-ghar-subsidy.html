Subsidy amounts

What the scheme pays, KW by KW.

The subsidy is calculated per kilowatt, not as a percentage. It is ₹30,000 for each of the first two KW and ₹18,000 for the third — and then it stops. ₹78,000 is the ceiling for a residential connection, no matter how large the system.

Eligibility

Who can claim it in Rajasthan.

The scheme is open to households across India, Rajasthan included. Four conditions decide whether your application goes through.

A domestic connection

You need a valid residential electricity connection in your name. Commercial and industrial connections are not eligible for this subsidy.

Rights to the roof

You own the roof, or you have written permission to install on it. Flats and housing societies apply through the society.

Shadow-free roof area

Roughly 100 sq ft per KW, clear of shade through the day. A 3 KW system needs about 300 sq ft.

A registered vendor

The install must be done by a vendor registered under the scheme, using DCR-compliant modules. We are empanelled — so this one is already handled.

Running a business instead? Commercial and industrial consumers don't get this subsidy, but they do get accelerated depreciation, which is often worth considerably more. See commercial solar →

Documents

Four papers. That's all.

People expect this to be a mountain of paperwork. It isn't. Applying on the portal costs nothing, and these are the only documents you need to have ready.

Latest electricity bill

We need the consumer number and your DISCOM name from it. Keep a photo of the full bill.

Aadhaar card

For identity verification on the national portal.

Bank passbook or cancelled cheque

The subsidy is credited straight into this account, so the name must match the connection holder.

Proof of roof ownership

A property document, or written permission from the owner if the roof isn't yours.

How to apply

Six steps — and we do five of them.

This is the full journey from application to subsidy landing in your account. In practice, the only step that needs you is the first one; we handle the rest as part of the installation.

  1. 01

    Register on the portal

    Register on the PM Surya Ghar national portal with your state, DISCOM and consumer number.

  2. 02

    Feasibility approval

    Your DISCOM checks technical feasibility and issues approval. We file this for you.

  3. 03

    Installation

    Our certified crew installs the system using DCR-compliant modules, as the scheme requires.

  4. 04

    Net meter application

    We submit the net-metering application with the installation details to your DISCOM.

  5. 05

    Inspection & commissioning

    The DISCOM inspects, fits the net meter and issues the commissioning certificate.

  6. 06

    Subsidy credited

    Bank details go up on the portal and the subsidy reaches your account — usually within about 30 days.

Realistic timeline: 6 to 10 weeks from application to subsidy credit. Most of that is DISCOM approval and net-meter fitting, which no installer can speed up — but knowing the real timeline up front is better than being told "two weeks" and then waiting two months.

Questions, answered

PM Surya Ghar, straight answers.

How much subsidy do I get under PM Surya Ghar Yojana?

₹30,000 per KW for the first 2 KW, plus ₹18,000 for the third KW. The maximum residential subsidy is ₹78,000, reached at a 3 KW system. Systems larger than 3 KW still receive ₹78,000 — the cap does not rise with size.

Who is eligible in Rajasthan?

Any household with a valid domestic electricity connection, rights to the roof and enough shadow-free area. The scheme is national, so Rajasthan households apply on the same portal and under the same rules as the rest of India.

What documents do I need?

Your latest electricity bill, Aadhaar card, a bank passbook or cancelled cheque, and proof of roof ownership. There is no fee to apply on the portal.

How long does the subsidy take to arrive?

Normally about 30 days after your DISCOM issues the commissioning certificate. End to end — from first application to money in your account — expect 6 to 10 weeks.

Is the subsidy deducted from the installer's price?

No. You pay the installer the full price, and the government credits the subsidy directly into your bank account after commissioning. Be cautious with any quote that shows the subsidy already deducted — that is not what you will need to pay on day one.

Can I take a loan for the rest?

Yes. Low-interest, collateral-free bank loans are available to households for rooftop solar under the scheme. For many families the EMI works out lower than the electricity bill the system replaces, which makes the system effectively self-funding from month one.

Why does my installer have to be a registered vendor?

Because the subsidy is only released for systems installed by a vendor registered under the scheme using DCR-compliant modules. If your installer isn't registered, the application cannot be processed and the subsidy is lost. Solar Energy Enterprises is empanelled under PM Surya Ghar, and has been registered and government-approved since 2018.

What happens to the extra power I generate?

It's exported to the grid through your net meter and credited against your consumption. Over a billing cycle you are charged only for the net units you actually drew — which is how bills fall by as much as 90%.
